- Proposal (`src/model.mjs`: `propose`, `acceptProposal`, `rejectProposal`; `POST /proposal`, `/proposal/accept`, `/proposal/reject`; `mycel propose <file>` in build format and/or `operations`, `title`): the batch is applied to the map at once, but added items are flagged `proposed: 'add'` (a new block gets `plan`), items changed in content are `change`, with the previous content and position in `graph.proposal`, and deletion is only flagged `remove` (deleting something that is proposed for adding happens at once). Accept: items flagged for deletion are deleted with everything nested, flags are cleared. ## Implemented in 0.10

- Hub (`server.mjs`, `src/hub.mjs`): one process for all projects. The registry `projects.json` (atomic write with a copy; if damaged, the copy, then a rebuild from `projects/*/project.json`), maps, chats and agents' read marks in `projects/<id>/`, the trash `trash/<id>/` for 7 days. The id is made from the name in Latin letters and is unique among projects, the trash and folders. Created through `projects/.new-<uuid>` and a rename; deletion: the route is switched to "deleting" (503 `retry`), the map and chat are closed, the folder is moved to the trash with retries, and only after the move does the project get `{deleted: true}` (the page goes to the home page, agents' waits get 404 deleted); if the folder is busy, 409, and the project keeps working. A project that failed to open is shown with an error and answers 503. The `server.lock` lock (JSON: pid, port, data, time); a stale one is taken over atomically through `server.lock.takeover`. Connecting: the same folder is the same project, a subfolder is the project above, a git repository inside a connected (or deleted) folder is its own project; the folder of a project the person deleted is refused without `--new`; `--project ID` moves a project to a new folder.

Step-by-step `build`: the final layout is computed locally (`applyOperations`), then written in steps with a 200 ms pause: first shifts of existing nodes and the new level, collapsed sub-maps whole, expanded ones one at a time; the person's edits between steps are printed at once, and `build` continues on top of them if they do not touch what is being built; repeating the same file skips what was written; the result is the same as `--at-once`.
- Layout (`src/layout.mjs`): levels on the chains of new nodes are processed from the deepest; a block that grew or got new notes makes room: blocks to the right move right, those below move down, as rigid groups by exactly the needed distance (gap 80/40 px), the notes of a grown block keep their side, new notes stack at the final edge; containers outside the chain are not touched, so collapsing after others' builds restores the map exactly; a safety pass removes new overlaps. `reflow()` for collapsing did not change.

A project has "‹ name" in the bottom bar, its own events, live updates with one request, new blocks and links fading in, following the build (turned off by your own move, "Show all" turns it on; if the whole map is smaller than 40 %, the concepts being built are fitted), token refresh on 401. ## Implemented in 0.7

- Agents: `mycel.mjs` with the commands `context`, `graph`, `changes`, `wait`, `apply`, `build`. The read revision is stored per agent in `<data folder>/agents/<actor>.json`; writes go only on top of it, and on 409 the command prints the changes. `wait` holds a long request and ends on the first content edit by someone else (by the user's decision, moves, size changes and collapsing do not wake it but arrive with the next edit; `--all` means any edit), and survives a server restart. Changes are printed in Markdown: History records, added and changed items in full, items that were only moved as a list, deleted items, links.
- Server: every History record has a delta (ids of added items, changed items with their fields, deleted items in brief). `GET /api/changes?since=N[&exclude=actor][&wait=S][&content=1]` merges the deltas, sets `contentChanged` and returns the current content of the affected nodes; `complete: false` if the history does not cover the range. A batch of up to 1000 operations.
- Automatic placement (`src/layout.mjs`): nodes without `x` and `y` are laid out after the batch: sub-maps from the deepest to the outer ones; on a level, columns by links (source to the left; a link to a nested block counts as a link to its sub-map), order within a column by the average height of the sources, without links an almost square grid, notes to the right of the block; the `layout` operation (after the user's note "it needs more order") re-orders a level, and with `deep` its sub-maps too; a container grows to its content with a 24 px margin, and the size of a block without `w` and `h` is fitted to its text.
- Sub-map: an expanded one stores its size and is no smaller than its content; a collapsed one is a compact card up to 280 px without a resize corner. "Block inside" on an ordinary block immediately gives a 520 × 300 field and puts the node in a free spot with a 24 px margin; if the node is not saved, the size goes back. Blocks inside move freely (while the pointer is over the field, not left of or above it), are dragged into the sub-map (the field is highlighted) and out of it onto the main map, together with their notes; containers that got crowded grow and save their size in the same record. The sub-map field is a plain backing (before requirement 24, a canvas with dots), and the dragged block lies above everything.

## Limitations and next steps

- The "what the agent is doing" line and the person's selection live in the hub's memory: a restart resets them. The agent learns about the person's events by waiting (`wait`/`mycel_wait`), not by a direct push into the session.

- A rejected proposal leaves the map, but neighbours pushed apart by what it added stay in their new places (as after deleting a block). A mark cannot be changed by hand in the interface; the agent sets it; one open proposal per project.

- Edits the person started and did not finish (closed the tab without "Finish edits") hold their records back from agents until they open the project and press the button: edits have no expiry. The agent sees them in `changes` with a note that the person is still editing.

- One block at a time: no multi-select and no copying; automatic placement covers agent blocks without coordinates and a level on the `layout` operation. Undo exists only for deleting a block, a note or a link.
- Text in a block is cut to the block's size; the size is set by hand (for agent blocks, fitted to the text).
- History is limited to the last 200 events; every move and resize takes one record.
- Mycel started in the background runs until `mycel.mjs stop` or a reboot; there is no stop button in the interface.
- A deleted project can be restored from the interface only with the "Undo" button while the notification is visible; after that it stays 7 days in `.mycel/trash/` (restore through `POST /api/projects/<id>/restore`).
- A large project map at the "Mechanics" level is wide (5424 px for `ural-logistics-sim`): it can be read in full only in focus on a concept; during a build the view follows the concepts being built.
- A note under a wide sub-map, further right than the width of the collapsed card (280 px), stays in place on collapsing, far below the card, and returns exactly; likewise a note below a block and to the right of its edge stays, if it does not continue the stack (is not on its line). Notes that already lie on their own block (as happened before pushing apart on growth existed) may be moved by the safety pass when the block collapses. Dragging a block onto a neighbour does not push anything apart by itself: only sub-map growth does.
- Collapsing can put one of a block's notes on another of its notes: a sticker on a corner onto a note above the block, a stack on the right onto a note under the right part of the sub-map (this happens if you drop a note under the middle of an agent's sub-map with two notes). The safety pass fixes this, and the moved note stays in its new place after expanding (in the example, 14 px lower); after that the chevron no longer moves it. A block that stands between a sub-map and that sub-map's own note (between the bottom edge and a note below it, between the right edge and a stack) lets the note pass by on collapsing, and on expanding the note pushes it away. Without storing previous positions, both cases cannot be told apart from ordinary ones (their collapsed map is the same as that of a map where the note or block stood in that place from the start), and it is the ordinary ones that return exactly.
- The chat is redrawn as a whole, so a click on a link in the chat can be lost while the agent is writing step lines.
- The demo map of past versions was not moved over (it is in the temporary folder of session 1539ba35, `scratchpad\kontur-data`).
- Before the next piece of work, read CONCEPT.md and git status. Change the map only through the API or `mycel.mjs`; do not write directly into `.mycel/` while Mycel is running, and do not include the token in commits.
